## 0. The one-paragraph version

The estate began as a provenance machine for Sanskrit texts, became a live
evidence surface, and is now — by two owner decisions in one day — a
**comparative formalisation programme**: Trika's structural commitments are
machine-checked, those structures are mapped into existing formalisms and
consciousness science with explicit verdicts, and essays are generated
structurally from the comparison records. The product target (one system that
answers about a work and a concept in one breath) is operational 4/4. The theory
target (Trika as source, not identity) has a formalisation ladder and two draft
papers. What remains is human: verdict review, witnesses, sign-off.

## 2. The three pillars, and how they interlock

```
PILLAR 1 — SURFACE            PILLAR 2 — EVIDENCE             PILLAR 3 — COMPARISON
one system, 4 turns           layers T1→L4, traces,           xmodel tensions + comparisons
openpatala + scholar +        trail, EVIDENCE registry        verdicts: fit/partial/incompatible
formal + treeka               (214 thm / 166 entries)         essays generated structurally
        │                             │                                │
        └──────── ids ────────────────┴─────────── anchors ────────────┘
                     everything resolves through the same ID grammar
```

The interlock is the point: a comparison essay cites a theorem that resolves to a
registry entry that resolves to a passage that resolves to a work. Break any hop
and the gate refuses the essay.

Measured today: workflow 4/4; 415 concepts; 11 tensions; 10 comparisons; 10/10
essays through the gate; suite 138 OK.

## 3. The six incompatibilities are the finding

A plan that aims at synthesis will be judged on whether the synthesis is real.
This plan aims at **specification** instead:

| Interlocutor | Obstruction | Verdict is defensible because |
|---|---|---|
| HOT | no state-consciousness layer | it names the missing signature and the additivity contradiction |
| IIT | no causal graph; Φ undefined | it distinguishes "not computed" from "not definable" |
| GWT | no competition or broadcast | it refuses the ordering-is-architecture equivocation |
| QRI | no qualia space or metric | it refuses count-is-geometry |
| Catuṣkoṭi | classical decidability | it names a proof-core commitment, not a gap |
| Yoneda | no morphisms | it names the exact object that does not exist |

**Rule for this phase:** a new comparison may add a `fit` only if it names the
shared formal object and the mapping. Otherwise it is `partial` at best.
